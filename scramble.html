How to Play Scramble

  • A scrambled word appears in the center.
  • Type your guess and press Spacebar or use Check Answer.
  • Press Enter to move to the next word.
  • Use Hint or press ~ to reveal a letter. You have two hints per word.
  • Use Surrender or press Delete to reveal the answer.
  • Press Tab to open or close this help panel.
  • Press Esc to return to the lobby.
