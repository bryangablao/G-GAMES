# 💬 ChatPH

**ChatPH** is a free social media web app where people can create posts, share updates, and send messages to each other, all in one place and right from the browser.

🔗 **Visit the app:** https://bryangablao.github.io/ChatPH/

---

## 📖 Overview

ChatPH is designed as a simple, friendly social platform. Users can share what's on their mind through posts and stay connected with others through messaging. It combines the two core parts of social media, a public feed and private conversations, in a clean and easy-to-use interface.

## 🎯 Purpose

The goal of ChatPH is to give people a place to:

- **Express themselves** by posting updates, thoughts, and moments
- **Stay connected** by chatting with friends and other users
- **Discover people** and see what others are sharing
- **Use it anywhere** on any device with a modern web browser, with nothing to install

## 👥 Who Is It For?

- Friends who want a simple place to post and chat
- Communities and groups who want to stay in touch
- Anyone looking for a lightweight social platform that works right in the browser
- Users who want to share updates without installing another app

## ✨ Features

- 📝 **Posting** – create posts and share them on the feed
- 📰 **Feed** – browse posts from other users
- 💬 **Messaging** – send messages to other users
- 👤 **User accounts and profiles** – a personal identity for each user
- 📱 **Responsive design** – works on phones, tablets, and computers
- 🎨 **Clean, easy-to-use interface** – simple navigation between the feed and your conversations
- 🆓 **Free to use** – no download or payment needed

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open https://bryangablao.github.io/ChatPH/
2. Create an account or log in
3. Set up your profile
4. Write and publish your first post
5. Browse the feed to see what others have shared
6. Open a conversation and message another user

## 🔒 Safety and Community Guidelines

To keep ChatPH a friendly place for everyone:

- Be respectful to other users
- Don't post spam, harassment, or harmful content
- Don't share private information about yourself or others
- Keep your account details to yourself

## 🌐 Compatibility

ChatPH works on modern browsers, including:

- Google Chrome
- Microsoft Edge
- Mozilla Firefox
- Safari
- Mobile browsers on Android and iOS

## ❓ FAQ

**Is ChatPH free?**
Yes. It is free to use.

**Do I need to install anything?**
No. ChatPH runs in your web browser.

**Can I use it on my phone?**
Yes. The website is designed to work on mobile devices as well as computers.

**Can I message other users?**
Yes. You can send messages to other users directly in the app.
