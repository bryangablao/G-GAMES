How to Play

  1. You play X, and the computer plays O.
  2. Tap or click an empty square to place X.
  3. Get three marks in a row, column, or diagonal to win.
  4. If all squares fill without a winner, the round is a draw.
  5. Unbeatable mode uses minimax strategy.
