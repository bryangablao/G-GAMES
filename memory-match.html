How to Play Memory Match

  1. Choose a difficulty and select New Game.
  2. Tap or click two cards to reveal their icons.
  3. If the icons match, the cards remain face up.
  4. If they differ, the cards flip back after a short delay.
  5. Match every pair using as few moves as possible.
