How to Play Dama

  1. White moves first. Regular pieces move one square diagonally forward.
  2. If a capture is available, you must capture by jumping over an opposing piece into the empty square beyond it.
  3. If another capture is available after a jump, continue jumping with the same piece.
  4. Reach the opposite back row to promote a piece into a king.
  5. Kings can move and capture diagonally in both directions.
  6. Win by capturing all opposing pieces or leaving your opponent with no legal moves.
  7. In Player vs AI mode, you play White and the AI plays Black.
