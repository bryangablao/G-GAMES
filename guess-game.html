How to Play Guess Game

  1. The game secretly selects a whole number from 0 to 1000.
  2. Enter your guess and press Guess or the Enter key.
  3. If your guess is too low, increase your next guess.
  4. If your guess is too high, decrease your next guess.
  5. Continue narrowing the range until you find the exact number.
