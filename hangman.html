How to Play Hangman

  1. A hidden word is selected automatically at the start of every round.
  2. Select letters using the on-screen keyboard or your physical keyboard.
  3. Correct letters are revealed in the word.
  4. Incorrect letters add one part to the Hangman drawing.
  5. You win by revealing the entire word before reaching six mistakes.
