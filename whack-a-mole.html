How to Play

  1. Select Easy, Normal, or Hard before starting.
  2. Press Start Game to begin the 30-second round.
  3. Tap or click the mole that pops out of a hole to earn 10 points.
  4. Only the visible mole scores. Missed taps reset your combo.
  5. Pause and resume whenever you need. Your best score is saved on this device.
