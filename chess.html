How to Play Chess

  1. Choose Player vs Player or Player vs AI.
  2. In AI mode, choose Easy, Normal, or Hard difficulty. You play White and the AI plays Black.
  3. Tap or click one of your pieces to highlight its legal moves, then choose a destination square.
  4. The game detects check, checkmate, stalemate, castling, en passant, and pawn promotion.
  5. On mobile, the board automatically scales to fit your screen.
