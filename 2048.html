How to Play 2048

  1. Use the Arrow keys, WASD, swipe gestures, or the mobile direction buttons.
  2. Every move slides all tiles in the selected direction.
  3. Two matching tiles merge when they collide.
  4. The merged tile value is added to your score.
  5. Reach 2048 to win, then continue playing if you want a higher score.
