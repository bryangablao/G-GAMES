By accessing or using the Service, you acknowledge that information may be processed as described in this Privacy Policy. This Policy applies to information collected through the Service and does not automatically apply to third-party websites, third-party services, external advertisements, social platforms, browser extensions, hosting providers, or other websites that may be linked from G-GAMES.

If a specific game or future G-GAMES feature introduces a different data practice, additional privacy information may be displayed at the point where that feature is used.

Information you provide voluntarily

  • Name, username, display name, or other identifier if a future account or profile feature is offered.
  • Email address or other contact information if you send us a message, submit feedback, report an issue, or request support.
  • Content you submit through forms, surveys, feedback fields, bug reports, support requests, or other communication features.

Section 03

Game and Interaction Data

G-GAMES is designed around casual mini games. Depending on how each game is coded, gameplay information may exist only temporarily in your browser, may be stored locally on your device, or may be processed by a server if a future online feature is introduced.

Examples of game-related information may include:

  • Scores, levels, move counts, completion status, streaks, attempts, reaction times, or elapsed time.
  • Game preferences, difficulty selections, sound settings, theme settings, or last-played game.
  • Saved progress stored using browser local storage or similar client-side storage.
  • Leaderboard entries if a future public leaderboard or competitive mode is introduced.

If gameplay data is stored only in your browser, clearing browser storage may permanently remove that information. G-GAMES may not be able to restore data that was never transmitted to or stored on a G-GAMES-controlled server.
