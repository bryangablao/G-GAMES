How to Play Space Invaders

  1. Move your ship left and right using A and D, the arrow keys, or the mobile controls.
  2. Fire using the Space key or the Fire button on touch devices.
  3. Destroy every alien to advance to the next wave.
  4. Avoid enemy shots. You lose one life when your ship is hit.
  5. If the alien formation reaches the defense line, the mission ends.
  6. Each new wave moves faster and fires more aggressively.

Your high score is stored locally in your browser.
