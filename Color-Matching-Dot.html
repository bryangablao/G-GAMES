How to Play Connect the Dots

  1. Press or click a colored dot, then drag through neighboring grid squares toward the matching dot.
  2. Paths cannot cross or overlap paths of another color.
  3. Drag backward over your own path to erase part of it and change direction.
  4. Connect every matching color pair and fill every grid square to complete the level.
  5. Difficulty increases by level, and you can also switch anytime by selecting Easy, Normal, Hard, Expert, or Master in the Difficulty Progression panel.
  6. All generated levels are created from a complete hidden solution, so every level is solvable.
