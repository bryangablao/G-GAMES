How to Play Color Matching Dot

  1. Look at the large target dot in the center.
  2. Tap or click the matching color from the choices below before the timer reaches zero.
  3. Correct answers advance you to the next level and build your combo.
  4. Wrong answers or timeouts remove one life. The run ends after all three lives are lost.
  5. Difficulty increases automatically: Easy 1–200, Normal 201–400, Hard 401–600, Expert 601–800, and Master 801–1000.
  6. Complete Level 1000 to finish the full challenge.
