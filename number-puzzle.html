How to Play Number Puzzle

  1. The board contains numbers 1 through 15 and one empty space.
  2. Tap a tile directly beside the empty space to slide it into that position.
  3. Continue moving tiles until every number is arranged from 1 to 15.
  4. Use the Hint button to highlight one movable tile when you need help.
  5. Try to finish using as few moves as possible.
