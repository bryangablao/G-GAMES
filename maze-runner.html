How to Play Maze Runner

  1. Start at the cyan square in the upper-left area of the maze.
  2. Move through open paths using WASD, arrow keys, swipes, or the on-screen direction buttons.
  3. Reach the green exit square to complete the run.
  4. Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard to change the maze size.
  5. Your best completion time is saved in the browser for each difficulty.
