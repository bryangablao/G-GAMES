How to Play Minesweeper

  1. Choose Beginner, Intermediate, or Expert.
  2. Tap or click a tile to reveal it. The first revealed tile and its neighbors are safe.
  3. A number indicates how many mines are in its eight surrounding tiles.
  4. Switch to Place Flags on touchscreens, or right-click on desktop, to mark suspected mines.
  5. Reveal every tile without a mine to win. Flags alone do not win the game.
  6. On desktop, double-click a revealed number to reveal adjacent unflagged tiles when the neighboring flag count matches.
