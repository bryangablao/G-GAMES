How to Play Typing Test

  1. Choose a test duration from 30 seconds up to 10 minutes.
  2. Start typing the displayed sentence. The timer begins on your first character.
  3. Correct characters are highlighted while incorrect characters are marked clearly.
  4. When you complete a sentence exactly, the next sentence loads automatically.
  5. When time expires, your final WPM, accuracy, and completed sentence count are shown.
