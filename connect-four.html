How to Play Connect Four

  1. Players take turns dropping one disc into any column that is not full.
  2. The disc falls into the lowest available space in that column.
  3. Red moves first, followed by Gold.
  4. The first player to connect four discs horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins.
  5. If all 42 spaces are filled with no winner, the round ends in a draw.
