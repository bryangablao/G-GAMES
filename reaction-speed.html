How to Play Reaction Speed

  1. Press Start Game or tap the reaction target to begin.
  2. Wait while the target is amber. The green signal appears after a random delay.
  3. Tap the green target as quickly as possible. On desktop, you can also press Space.
  4. Tapping too early counts as a false start and cancels the round.
  5. Play again to improve your best time. Your best result is saved in this browser.
