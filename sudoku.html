How to Play Sudoku

Complete the puzzle using logic and careful elimination.

  1. Fill the grid so every row contains the numbers 1 through 9.
  2. Every column must also contain the numbers 1 through 9.
  3. Each 3×3 sector must contain the numbers 1 through 9.
  4. Pre-filled numbers cannot be changed.
  5. Use New Game for a new puzzle, Reset to clear your entries, or Surrender to reveal the solution.
