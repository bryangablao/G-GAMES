How to Play Flappy Bird

  1. Press Start Game to begin the round.
  2. Tap, click, press Space, or press Arrow Up to make the bird flap upward.
  3. Guide the bird through the opening between each pair of pipes.
  4. You earn one point for every pipe pair you successfully pass.
  5. The game ends if the bird touches a pipe, the top edge, or the ground.
