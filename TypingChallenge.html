How to Play Typing Challenge

  1. Choose Typing Test or Typing Quest.
  2. For Typing Test, select time or word mode and choose the round length.
  3. Start typing the displayed words. Correct letters brighten while mistakes are marked.
  4. Your result shows WPM, accuracy, raw speed, consistency, characters, and time.
  5. In Typing Quest, type the word above an enemy to attack it and defeat the boss.
  6. Press Tab at any time to restart the current mode.
