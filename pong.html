How to Play Pong

  1. Move your paddle up and down to return the ball.
  2. The first side to reach 7 points wins the match.
  3. In Player vs AI mode, use W/S or Arrow Up/Down.
  4. In Two Players mode, Player 1 uses W/S and Player 2 uses Arrow Up/Down.
  5. On mobile, drag vertically on the court or use the Move Up and Move Down buttons.
  6. Press P or use the Pause button to pause or resume the match.
