How to Play Color Match

  1. A color word appears in the center of the game.
  2. The word itself may name a different color than the ink used to display it.
  3. Choose the button that matches the actual ink color.
  4. Build a streak by answering correctly several times in a row.
  5. Finish the selected number of questions to complete the round.
