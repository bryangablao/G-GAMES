How to Play Snake

  • Press New Game to start a round.
  • Desktop: use the arrow keys to change direction.
  • Mobile and tablet: swipe on the board or use the on-screen directional controls.
  • Collect the glowing food to grow and increase your score.
  • Avoid the walls and your own body.
  • Use Pause when you need to temporarily stop the round.
