How to Play Brick Breaker

  1. Move the paddle left or right to keep the ball from falling below the screen.
  2. Press Space, tap the play area, or use the LAUNCH button to release the ball.
  3. Each brick you destroy adds to your score.
  4. Clear every brick to advance to the next level, where the ball moves faster.
  5. You have three lives. Losing all lives ends the run.
