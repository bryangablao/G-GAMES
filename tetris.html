How to Play Tetris

  • Move left or right with the arrow keys, swipe gestures, or the on-screen buttons.
  • Rotate with Z, X, tap, or the Rotate button.
  • Soft drop with Arrow Down or the Down button.
  • Hard drop with Space, swipe up, or the Drop button.
  • Hold a piece with C or the Hold button.
  • Pause or resume with P or the Pause button.
